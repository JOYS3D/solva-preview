    • How big is it, in centimetres?

    • Is the coat matted, or is this a maintenance groom?
    • Has he been groomed before?
    • Is he happy being handled?

    A matted coat is a longer slot. It flags that rather than booking a standard one.

    Restaurant
    • What time, and is it an occasion?
    • Any allergies?
    • Any steps we should avoid?

    Front of house finds out about the cake and the coeliac before service, not during it.

    • ✓Answer every call, at any hour, as your business
