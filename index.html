Straight answers

The limits matter more than the promises.

Anyone selling you a phone assistant that never needs a human is selling you a problem.

It will always

  • Answer every call, at any hour, in your business’s name
  • Ask the questions you agreed, in your words
  • Separate an emergency from a price enquiry, and route each where you said
  • Work with the diary you already use — writing the booking in where your system allows it, lodging it for you to confirm where it doesn’t
  • Say plainly that it’s an AI, every time it’s asked
  • Hand you anything it couldn’t answer, word for word

It won’t ever

  • Quote a price you haven’t set
  • Book outside the rules you gave it — hours, job types, deposits
  • Improvise an answer it doesn’t have
  • Make outbound calls. Inbound only, never cold-calling
  • Replace the conversation a difficult customer needs with you
  • Pretend to be a person
