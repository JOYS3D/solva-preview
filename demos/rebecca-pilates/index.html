03 — BEFORE YOU BEGIN

A few things
you might be wondering.

A comfortable starting point begins with a clear conversation.

Do I need to have done Pilates before?+

You can start by sharing your experience and what you’d like from a practice. The first conversation is a chance to discuss a suitable starting point and any adaptations you may need.

What would we talk about in health coaching?+

Your goals, your current routines and what feels realistic for you. The focus is on finding practical next steps for everyday wellbeing, rather than changing everything at once.

Can I explore Pilates and coaching together?+

Yes — choose “A little of both” in your enquiry. It’s a useful way to start a conversation about how movement and everyday habits could support the goals you have in mind.

What happens after I enquire?+

The proposed next step is a personal reply to discuss your goals, session options and availability. This website is a design preview: enquiries aren’t sent and no appointments are booked.
